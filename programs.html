OUR WORK

Building resilient and
sustainable communities.

NRI works with communities in Northern Kenya to improve access to water, promote education, empower women and youth, protect the environment, and strengthen livelihoods through sustainable community development initiatives.

01

WATER SECURITY & CLIMATE RESILIENCE

Safe water and climate readiness.

Recurring drought and water scarcity shape daily life across Northern Kenya. We strengthen access to water and community capacity to adapt.

  • Rainwater harvesting systems
  • Water storage infrastructure
  • Community water access initiatives
  • Drought preparedness and awareness
  • Tree planting and environmental conservation
  • School water access support
02

WOMEN’S EMPOWERMENT

Skills, income and leadership.

Supporting women — including vulnerable women and widows — to build livelihoods and take part in decision-making.

  • Skills development and entrepreneurship
  • Savings and income-generating groups
  • Leadership development
  • Support for vulnerable women and widows
03

GIRLS’ EDUCATION & PROTECTION

Keeping girls in school and safe.

Education and protection initiatives that help girls stay in school and thrive.

  • Educational support initiatives
  • Menstrual hygiene awareness and support
  • Mentorship and school retention programs
  • Advocacy against harmful practices
04

YOUTH DEVELOPMENT

Skills for work and leadership.

Preparing young people with practical skills, confidence and pathways to opportunity.

  • Mentorship and life skills training
  • Entrepreneurship support
  • Leadership development
  • Employment readiness initiatives
05

COMMUNITY RESILIENCE & WELL-BEING

Prepared communities, stronger households.

Supporting communities to respond to shocks and protect the most vulnerable.

  • Emergency drought response initiatives
  • Health and nutrition awareness
  • Support for vulnerable households
  • Community resilience strengthening programs

INITIAL PROGRAMME FOCUS

Starting where the need is greatest.

As a newly established organization, NRI is initially focusing on improving water security and strengthening climate resilience through:

  • Installation of water storage tanks
  • Rainwater harvesting projects
  • Community drought preparedness and awareness
  • Tree planting and environmental conservation initiatives
  • School water access support

Through these initiatives, NRI aims to improve access to safe and reliable water, strengthen communities’ ability to adapt to climate change, and enhance quality of life across Northern Kenya.
