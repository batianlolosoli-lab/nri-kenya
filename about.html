WHO WE ARE

Driven by community.
Sustained by resilience.

Northern Resilience Initiatives is a community-based organization dedicated to improving the lives of people in Northern Kenya through education, water security, women’s and youth empowerment, environmental conservation, and sustainable livelihoods.

OUR PURPOSE

Empowering local communities with lasting solutions.

We work closely with local communities to identify their needs and implement sustainable initiatives that create lasting positive change.

Northern Kenya continues to face recurring drought, water scarcity, and the effects of climate change. These challenges disproportionately affect women, children, youth, and vulnerable households. Our programmes help communities address these pressures and build a more sustainable future.

Mission

To work alongside communities in Northern Kenya to improve lives through education, water security, youth and women’s empowerment, environmental conservation, and sustainable development initiatives that create lasting positive change.

Vision

To see thriving communities in Northern Kenya empowered to overcome challenges, harness opportunities, and build a sustainable future for present and future generations.

“Resilient Communities, Brighter Futures.”
